Research

My doctoral work forms a perception-to-feedback control loop with a human in the loop: sensing movement, scoring it against a reference, and acting on the result while the person is still moving. I am extending this toward human-centred, data-driven control for physical human–robot interaction — models of human motor behaviour that a controller can rely on for assistance, rehabilitation, and shared-autonomy tasks.
